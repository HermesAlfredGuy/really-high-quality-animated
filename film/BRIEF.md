# BRIEF — The Little Lighthouse

FILM       A tiny desk lamp practices being a lighthouse, loses its bulb in a huff, and finds the moon
           was shining on it the whole time.
AUDIENCE   the owner; anyone who likes a 50-second hand-inked cartoon with a small joke and a warm ending.
DURATION   50 s (24 fps, 1920x1080). Music-led, one narrator.
FORMATS    16:9 master + phone-friendly share copy (<= 45 MB).
REAL       nothing from a product: this is a drawn cartoon. Every frame is code-generated cel animation
           (canvas 2D ink + paper), no stock footage, no AI images.
REFERENCE  1) mg-styles-15 / 05-cel-boil (in-house): hand-inked cel look, 3-drawing line boil, exposure
              on twos, paper grain, off-register flat colour. Borrow the whole drawing engine feel.
           2) UPA / 1950s limited animation + Pixar "Luxo Jr." acting: posture-only character, no face.
           3) Charley Harper / mid-century screenprint: flat shapes, limited ink, textured stock.
REJECT     - a static or slideshow film (motion gate median < 2.5, any dead run > 2 s)
           - centred text cards doing the storytelling; the STORY must be acted by the lamp
           - a smooth digital vector look (no boil, no grain, no paper) or gradients doing the shading
           - lip-sync puppetry or a face pasted on the lamp: act through posture, light and the cut
           - music that ignores the picture: every cut and the bulb pop land on a real beat
SOUND      House library bed (CC BY/CC0, credited): playful/warm cue, cut on the bar grid.
           Narrator: 4 short lines, Chatterbox (EN), mixed ~-19 LUFS under the music.
           SFX: bulb click ON, filament ping, bulb POP, wing flutter, desk knocks, dust shimmer.
